GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

Hi! I’m 23 years old and based in the Philippines (PHT). I’ve been developing games for around 2 years, and along the way, I’ve also gained experience testing Roblox games and helping developers improve them.

I’ve had the chance to test several games that went on to reach millions of visits and thousands of concurrent players. For some of them, I was the only playtester, so I handled the testing myself and shared the issues and improvements I found along the way.

When I test a game, I try to be as honest as possible. If something feels off, doesn’t work properly, or makes the gameplay less enjoyable, I’ll say so. I don’t believe in sugarcoating feedback just to make it sound nice. Developers need to know what’s actually wrong so they can fix it.

Some of my suggestions have also been used and implemented by the developers I’ve worked with. That’s what makes QA testing meaningful to me. I want to help developers catch problems early and make their games better for the people who play them.

HOW I WORK

My Testing Process

Once the payment is completed, I’ll spend a 2-hour testing session exploring your game, its features, gameplay, and overall experience. After the session, I’ll put together a detailed QA report covering the bugs I found, any issues worth looking into, and suggestions for improvement.

Once you’ve received the report, you can ask me questions or discuss any of the findings with me. And for the second session, we’ll follow the same process as the first one.
